Gamma walls verdict (the Gamma walls chip value, refreshed each morning gamma run — it reads the morning wall migration, not the time): chased = walls moved with price, options repositioning, the trend has structure · diverged = walls moved against price, the move is not sponsored by options flow, reversion risk · shakeout = price moved broadly but walls held, a spot-only move, the old walls still define the range · leading = walls moved together while price stayed flat, new open interest building ahead of price, the new wall is the magnet · mixed = no clear majority, walls split direction · quiet = little price or wall movement.
