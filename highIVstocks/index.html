ONER = Option Notional Exposure Ratio = (total open interest × 100 × price) ÷ market cap. Measures option-market notional exposure relative to company size.
Prem% = (ATM call time-value + ATM put time-value) ÷ 2, as % of spot, nearest Friday expiry.
PW distance % = (price − put wall) ÷ put wall. Green = above put wall, red = below.
Strip bar: drag across a strip's bar to read the price at any point along it. Tap a strip to open its chart; tap the news line to switch between news and levels.
Fear & Greed: 0–24 extreme fear · 25–44 fear · 45–55 neutral · 56–74 greed · 75–100 extreme greed. History-bar colors run on a continuous scale, deep red at 0 to deep green at 100.
SI% = shares sold short ÷ float shares, from FINRA's twice-monthly short-interest file (settlement mid-month + month-end, published ~7 business days later). The number after the dash is days to cover.
INST% = shares held by institutions ÷ float shares, from Nasdaq's 13F-derived institutional holdings (quarterly, ~45-day lag). Can exceed 100% on heavily shorted names — the same shares get counted in multiple holders' long positions.
