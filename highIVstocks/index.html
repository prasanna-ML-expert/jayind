Gamma wall changes
Only wall moves ≥ 2.5% shown · PCR shown on a move ≥ 0.25 or a cross of 1.0 (red = PCR up / put-heavy build, green = PCR down) · — = no substantial change · green = wall up, red = wall down
SPX / Nasdaq news
News score = blended %pos − %neg (60% index items / 40% stock notes) · one bar per day · green positive, red negative
EMA8 percentile breadth
Names at ≥ 70th percentile of candle extension vs EMA8 (full-history percentrank, above/below EMA8 counted separately) · green = above EMA8 · red = below EMA8 · Dist % = close vs EMA8 · tap Pctl or Dist % to sort
EMA8 stack strength
Strongest first. Sequence: weekly EMA8 > monthly EMA8, then daily EMA8 > weekly EMA8, then price > daily EMA8 · within a tier, ranked by the spreads in the same order (Wk vs Mo, then Dy vs Wk, then Px vs Dy) · cells show the signed % spread (green = condition met, red = not)
Fear & Greed history
One bar per price refresh, oldest → newest · CNN Fear & Greed · zones: extreme fear 0–24 · fear 25–44 · neutral 45–55 · greed 56–74 · extreme greed 75–100 · last 180 updates
PCR history
PCR = put open interest ÷ call open interest · pink ≥ 1.0 (put-heavy) · blue < 1.0 (call-heavy) · dashed line = 1.0 · one bar per day · last 90 days
ONER = Option Notional Exposure Ratio = (total open interest × 100 × price) ÷ market cap. Measures option-market notional exposure relative to company size.
Prem% = (ATM call time-value + ATM put time-value) ÷ 2, as % of spot, nearest Friday expiry.
PCR = put open interest ÷ call open interest (>1 put-heavy, <1 call-heavy). Tap a strip's PCR badge to see its daily history (last 90 days).
PW distance % = (price − put wall) ÷ put wall. Green = above put wall, red = below.
Strip bar: drag across a strip's bar to read the price at any point along it. Tap a strip's bar to open its chart; tap the news line to switch between news and levels.
Fear & Greed: 0–24 extreme fear · 25–44 fear · 45–55 neutral · 56–74 greed · 75–100 extreme greed. History-bar colors run on a continuous scale, deep red at 0 to deep green at 100.
SI% = shares sold short ÷ float shares, from FINRA's twice-monthly short-interest file (settlement mid-month + month-end, published ~7 business days later). The number after the dash is days to cover.
INST% = shares held by institutions ÷ float shares, from Nasdaq's 13F-derived institutional holdings (quarterly, ~45-day lag). Can exceed 100% on heavily shorted names — the same shares get counted in multiple holders' long positions.
